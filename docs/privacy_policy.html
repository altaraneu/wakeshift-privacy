Privacy Policy – WakeShift

Last updated: November 2026

WakeShift ("the app") is a shift-based alarm application published under the developer account altaran. This policy describes how the app handles data.

The app is developed by an independent developer based in the European Union. The developer has no interest in collecting or tracking users. No user data is gathered for analytics, advertising, or any other purpose beyond what is strictly necessary for the app to work. There is no account and no sign-in.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following data only on your device (local database and settings):

  • Alarms, shift templates, schedules (shift cycles and day patterns), vacations and exceptions you create
  • Settings such as language, time format, first day of week, holiday country and snooze duration
  • Your choices for alarm sound and vibration
  • Running timers and the stopwatch
  • If you register them for a wake-up challenge: the content of a QR code and the identifier of an NFC tag, used only to check that you scanned the same code or tag
  • A technical debug log (times, alarm identifiers, settings and errors; never text you type), kept on the device and overwritten over time

This data is not sent to the developer or any third party, except when you choose to send a bug report (see below). It stays on your phone or tablet, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Camera, motion sensors and NFC

  • Camera: used only when you choose the QR code challenge, to scan your registered code. The images are processed on your device and are not saved or sent anywhere. Scanning uses Google's ML Kit library, which runs on the device; Google may receive anonymous technical information from it (such as device model, app version and performance data) to keep the library working, as described in ML Kit's terms.
  • Physical activity (step counting): used only when you choose the steps challenge, to count steps while an alarm is ringing. Steps are counted on your device by its step sensor and are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Motion sensor: the shake challenge, and the emergency override that lets you skip a challenge by shaking the phone for a long time, read the accelerometer only while an alarm is ringing. Nothing is stored or sent.
  • NFC: used only when you choose the NFC challenge, to read your registered tag while an alarm is ringing.

The app is not a health or fitness app and does not keep any health data.

Bug reports

If you tap "Report a bug" in Settings, the app prepares an e-mail to the developer in your own e-mail app. It contains the debug log, the app version, your device model and Android version, and, only if you tick the option, a copy of your alarm database (alarm names, shift templates and schedules). Nothing is sent unless you send the e-mail yourself. The developer uses the report only to find and fix the problem and deletes it when it is no longer needed.

Network use

  • Holiday dates: If you choose a holiday country in Settings, the app requests public holiday dates from the external service date.nager.at. Only the country code and year are used in the request (e.g. CZ, 2026). No personal data is sent. Results are cached on your device for offline use.
  • In-app purchases: Purchases and donations are processed by Google Play. Payment and billing data are handled according to Google's Privacy Policy. The app does not receive or store your payment details; it only learns whether a purchase is active.

No analytics, ads or tracking

The app does not use analytics, advertising or third-party tracking. The developer does not collect personal data about you through the app.

Permissions

The app requests permissions needed for alarms and its optional features: notifications, exact alarms, full-screen alarm screen, running after a restart, vibration, a foreground service while an alarm rings or a timer runs, and, only when you use the matching challenge, camera, physical activity and NFC. These are used only for the app's features and not to collect or send data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you control it directly: you can edit or delete it in the app, or remove it completely by uninstalling the app. If you sent a bug report and want it deleted, contact the developer.

Changes

This policy may be updated occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top will be revised when changes are made. Continued use of the app after updates constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or the app, you can contact the developer through the app's listing on Google Play (altaran / WakeShift).
