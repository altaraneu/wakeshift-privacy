Privacy Policy – WakeShift

Last updated: February 2025

WakeShift ("the app") is a shift-based alarm application published under the developer account altaran. This policy describes how the app handles data.

The app is developed by an independent developer based in the European Union. The developer has no interest in collecting or tracking users. No user data is gathered for analytics, advertising, or any other purpose beyond what is strictly necessary for the app to function (e.g. local storage on your device and, if you use the feature, fetching public holiday dates by country).

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following data only on your device (local database and settings):

  • Alarms, shift templates, schedules (shift cycles), and exceptions you create
  • Settings such as language, 24-hour format, first day of week, holiday country, postpone duration
  • Your choices for alarm sound and vibration

This data is not sent to the developer or any third party. It stays on your phone or tablet.

Network use

  • Holiday dates: If you choose a holiday country in Settings, the app may request public holiday dates from the external service date.nager.at. Only the country code and year are used in the request (e.g. CZ, 2025). No personal data is sent. Results may be cached on your device for offline use.
  • In-app purchases: Purchases and donations are processed by Google Play. Payment and billing data are handled according to Google's Privacy Policy. The app does not receive or store your payment details.

No analytics or tracking

The app does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking. The developer does not collect personal data about you through the app.

Permissions

The app requests permissions needed for alarms (notifications, full-screen intent for the alarm screen, exact alarm, etc.). These are used only to deliver alarm functionality and are not used to collect or send data.

Changes

This policy may be updated occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top will be revised when changes are made. Continued use of the app after updates constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or the app, you can contact the developer through the app's listing on Google Play (altaran / WakeShift).
